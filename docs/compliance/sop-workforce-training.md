**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This SOP establishes requirements for HIPAA Security and Privacy awareness
training for all InfinityRx workforce members who access, handle, or support
systems that store or process ePHI. Training compliance is a condition of
continued system access.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to:
- All full-time and part-time employees
- Contractors and temporary workers with system access
- Vendors with BAA-covered system access
- Business Associates' employees with InfinityRx platform access

---

## 3. Training Requirements

### 3.1 New-Hire Training (Before Access Granted)

All new hires must complete the following before system access is provisioned:

| Module | Duration | Medium | Required Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| HIPAA Privacy Rule Fundamentals | 60 min | Online self-paced | 80% |
| HIPAA Security Rule Essentials | 60 min | Online self-paced | 80% |
| PHI Handling and Minimum Necessary | 30 min | Online self-paced | 80% |
| Password and MFA Security | 30 min | Online self-paced | 80% |
| Breach Recognition and Reporting | 30 min | Online self-paced | 80% |
| InfinityRx Platform Security Policies | 45 min | Online self-paced | 80% |

Total: approximately 4.5 hours. Completion within 5 business days of start date.

### 3.2 Annual Refresh Training

All workforce members must complete annual refresh training by December 31
of each calendar year:

| Module | Duration | Medium | Required Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual HIPAA Security Update | 30 min | Online self-paced | 80% |
| Annual Privacy Practices Review | 30 min | Online self-paced | 80% |
| Current Threat Landscape | 15 min | Online self-paced | Pass/Fail |
| Policy Changes (if any) | Variable | Online self-paced | 80% |

### 3.3 Role-Specific Training

Certain roles require additional training:

| Role | Additional Training | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| `fwa_investigator` | FWA detection ethics and procedures | Annual |
| `billing_analyst` | Financial data handling, CMS regulations | Annual |
| `pharmacist` | Clinical record access responsibilities | Annual |
| `super_admin` | Privileged access procedures, break-glass policy | Biannual |
| Software Engineers | Secure coding, HIPAA-compliant development | Annual |

### 3.4 Triggered Training

The following events require completion of targeted training within 30 days:
- Participation in a security incident (involved party)
- Policy change affecting the workforce member's role
- Regulatory update (e.g., new CMS guidance)
- Failure of phishing simulation
- First-time access grant to a new ePHI system

---

## 4. Training Delivery

### 4.1 Platform

Training is delivered via the InfinityRx Learning Management System (LMS).
All completions, scores, and attestations are automatically logged.

### 4.2 Attestation

After completing each training module, the workforce member must digitally
attest: "I have read and understand the policies covered in this module and
agree to comply."

### 4.3 Failed Attempts

- First failure: Workforce member may retake immediately.
- Second failure: Manager notified; 48-hour cooling-off period before retake.
- Third failure: HR review; access suspended pending successful completion.

---

## 5. Tracking and Reporting

### 5.1 Records Retention

Training completion records are retained for 6 years per 45 CFR §164.316(b)(2).
Records include:
- Workforce member name and ID
- Training module name and version
- Completion date and score
- Attestation timestamp and IP address

### 5.2 Compliance Reporting

The Privacy Officer reviews a training compliance dashboard monthly:
- Completion rates by department
- Overdue completions (> 30 days past due)
- Failed completion trend

Departments with < 90% completion rate receive written notice.
Departments with < 80% completion rate after 30 days trigger escalation to
executive leadership.

### 5.3 Contractor and Vendor Training

Vendors and contractors must provide their own HIPAA training completion
certificates covering equivalent content, OR complete InfinityRx training.
Certificates are retained with the BAA documentation.

---

## 6. Non-Compliance Consequences

Per the InfinityRx Workforce Sanctions Policy:
- First offense (late completion, < 30 days): Written reminder.
- Second offense: Verbal warning; access suspended until completion.
- Willful non-compliance or repeated failure: Up to and including termination.

Sanctions are applied consistently without regard to role level (including management).

---

## 7. Training Content Review

Training content is reviewed and updated:
- Annually by the Privacy Officer and Security Officer.
- Within 60 days of any material HIPAA regulatory change.
- After any significant security incident affecting the platform.

Content version history is maintained in the LMS.
